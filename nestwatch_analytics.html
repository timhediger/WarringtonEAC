Pennsylvania Bluebird Data — NestWatch vs BSP 2025

Comparison of the two primary sources of Pennsylvania Eastern Bluebird data in 2025. These two programs operate largely independently — BSP members typically report only to BSP, and NestWatch participants report only to Cornell. There is very little overlap between the two monitor communities. This means their fledgling counts are essentially additive: the combined estimate of ~9,776 fledglings is a much more complete picture of PA bluebird production than either source alone. The near-identical average fledglings per box (2.82 vs 2.85) is a reassuring consistency check — both programs are capturing similar nesting quality despite operating at very different scales.
